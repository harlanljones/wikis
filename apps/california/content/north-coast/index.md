# North Coast

North of the Golden Gate, Highway 1 clings to the cliffs for two hundred miles and then gives up. This is the wet end of California: fog all summer, storms all winter, and in places more than a hundred inches of rain a year. It grows the tallest trees on Earth.

It has always been hard to reach. The Russians got here before the Americans. Lumber left by sea from tiny cliffside ports because there were no roads. One stretch of coast was so rugged that the highway engineers went around it, and it has been called the Lost Coast ever since.
