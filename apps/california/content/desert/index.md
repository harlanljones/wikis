# The Desert

A quarter of California is desert, and it is three different deserts.

- The **Mojave** is the high desert, mostly between 2,000 and 5,000 feet. It has cold winters, hot summers and Joshua trees. [[desert/death-valley|Death Valley]] is a deep hole in its northern end.
- The **Colorado Desert**, in the southeast corner, is a piece of the Sonoran Desert. It is low, often below sea level, and hotter, with no hard frost. It grows ocotillo, palo verde and fan palms.
- The **Great Basin** desert, cold sagebrush country, reaches into the state east of the Sierra. That is where you find [[eastern-sierra/mono-lake|Mono Lake]] and [[eastern-sierra/bodie|Bodie]].

All three exist for one reason. Storms come off the Pacific and have to climb the Sierra Nevada, the Transverse Ranges or the Peninsular Ranges. Rising air cools and drops its water on the western slopes. What comes down the other side is dry. Death Valley sits behind four mountain ranges in a row and gets about two inches of rain a year.

The desert only looks empty. People have lived here for more than 10,000 years, since the days when the dry lake beds were full of water and fish.
