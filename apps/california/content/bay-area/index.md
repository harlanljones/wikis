# Around the Bay

The hike list starts at home, and home turns out to be one of the stranger pieces of real estate in North America. The Bay itself is a river valley that drowned when the ice ages ended. The hills on either side of it are scraped-off seafloor. One of the planet's great faults runs straight through the suburbs, and a slab of granite from Southern California is sliding past Marin on its way to Alaska.

People have lived well here for a very long time. Ohlone and Coast Miwok villages ringed the Bay for thousands of years before Spanish ships found the Golden Gate in 1775, and the shellmounds they built were once the tallest structures on the shore.
