# Hike List

Every hike on the California Hike List, in the same order, with the reading that goes with it. Several hikes share a reading when they are in the same park.

Distances, permits and closures are on the hike list itself and on each park's own page. This wiki is for the stories.

## By theme

The hike list sorts trails into six themes. These readings go deepest on each.

- **Redwoods and big trees:** [[big-picture/three-big-trees|Three Big Trees]], then [[north-coast/redwood-national-park|Redwood National Park]], [[sierra-nevada/giant-forest|Giant Forest]] and [[eastern-sierra/bristlecone-pines|The Bristlecone Pines]].
- **Coast and cliffs:** [[bay-area/point-reyes|Point Reyes]], [[central-coast/big-sur|Big Sur]], [[north-coast/lost-coast|The Lost Coast]] and [[south-coast/channel-islands|Channel Islands]].
- **Peaks and volcanoes:** [[big-picture/how-california-was-built|How California Was Built]], then [[volcano-country/lassen|Lassen]], [[central-coast/pinnacles|Pinnacles]], [[sierra-nevada/half-dome|Half Dome]] and [[eastern-sierra/mount-whitney|Mount Whitney]].
- **Waterfalls and lakes:** [[big-picture/water|Water]], then [[sierra-nevada/yosemite-valley|Yosemite Valley]], [[volcano-country/burney-falls|Burney Falls]], [[sierra-nevada/lake-tahoe|Lake Tahoe]] and [[eastern-sierra/mono-lake|Mono Lake]].
- **Desert:** [[desert|The Desert]], then [[desert/death-valley|Death Valley]], [[desert/joshua-tree|Joshua Tree]] and [[desert/anza-borrego|Anza-Borrego]].
- **History and heritage:** [[big-picture/first-peoples|First Peoples]] and [[big-picture/gold-rush|The Gold Rush]], then [[bay-area/angel-island|Angel Island]], [[gold-country/coloma|Coloma]], [[volcano-country/lava-beds|Lava Beds]] and [[eastern-sierra/manzanar|Manzanar]].
