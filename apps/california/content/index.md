# California on Foot

Fun readings to go with the California Hike List: the geography and history behind every trail on it, from the redwoods in the Oakland hills to the top of Mount Whitney.

Each page takes one place on the list and answers the questions that come up on the trail. Why is this rock red? Who lived here? Why is there a ghost town, a waterfall, a fort or a five-thousand-year-old tree in this spot? Read one the night before a hike, or in the car on the way.
