# Santa Cruz Mountains and Central Coast

South of the Bay the coast gets steeper and emptier. The Santa Cruz Mountains hold the redwoods that started California's conservation movement. Beyond Monterey the Santa Lucia Range drops into the sea at Big Sur, and inland, across the San Andreas Fault, half of a volcano sits in the dry hills near Hollister.

This is also mission country. The Spanish road north, El Camino Real, ran up the Salinas Valley, and Gaspar de Portolá's expedition stumbled through here in 1769 looking for Monterey Bay and finding redwoods, grizzlies and San Francisco Bay instead.
