# South Coast and Islands

Southern California's coast faces south as much as west. Past Point Conception the shoreline turns a corner, the water warms up, and the mountains run east to west instead of north to south. Offshore lies a chain of islands that were never connected to the mainland and evolved their own animals.

This is also where the written history of California starts. The first Europeans to see the coast landed here in 1542, and the oldest known human remains in the state come from the islands.
