# Timeline

Everything in the wiki, in order. Follow a link for the story.

## Deep time

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| 1.7 billion years ago | The oldest rock in the state forms, now exposed on Ryan Mountain at [[desert/joshua-tree\|Joshua Tree]] |
| 200 to 85 million years ago | An ocean plate dives under the continent. The Sierra granite and the gold veins form. See [[big-picture/how-california-was-built\|How California Was Built]] |
| 50 million years ago | Rivers lay down gold-bearing gravels in the foothills, later mined at [[gold-country/malakoff-diggins\|Malakoff Diggins]]. A lagoon leaves the layers at [[south-coast/torrey-pines\|Torrey Pines]] |
| 30 million years ago | The San Andreas Fault system begins |
| 23 million years ago | A volcano erupts near Lancaster. Half of it becomes [[central-coast/pinnacles\|Pinnacles]] |
| 10 to 5 million years ago | The Sierra tilts and rises. [[desert/death-valley\|Death Valley]] and [[sierra-nevada/lake-tahoe\|Tahoe]] begin to drop |
| 760,000 years ago | The Long Valley eruption. [[eastern-sierra/mono-lake\|Mono Lake]] already exists |
| 100,000 years ago | Lava pools and cracks into columns at [[eastern-sierra/devils-postpile\|Devils Postpile]] |
| 27,000 years ago | The dome of [[volcano-country/lassen\|Lassen Peak]] rises |
| 20,000 years ago | Peak of the last ice age. Glaciers fill [[sierra-nevada/yosemite-valley\|Yosemite]]. Sea level is 400 feet lower |
| 13,000 years ago | People are living on the [[south-coast/channel-islands\|Channel Islands]]. Mammoths and ground sloths die out soon after |
| 10,000 to 8,000 years ago | The sea floods through the Golden Gate and makes San Francisco Bay. See [[bay-area/lands-end\|Lands End]] |
| About 2850 BC | The oldest living [[eastern-sierra/bristlecone-pines\|bristlecone pine]] sprouts |
| About 1200 BC | The oldest living [[sierra-nevada/giant-forest\|giant sequoias]] sprout |

## Spain and Mexico

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| 1542 | Juan Rodríguez Cabrillo lands at San Diego. See [[south-coast/cabrillo\|Cabrillo and Point Loma]] |
| 1579 | Francis Drake repairs his ship, probably at [[bay-area/point-reyes\|Point Reyes]] |
| 1602 to 1603 | Sebastián Vizcaíno names [[central-coast/ano-nuevo\|Año Nuevo]], Point Reyes and San Diego |
| 1769 | The Portolá expedition and Father Junípero Serra found the first mission, at San Diego. The expedition reaches San Francisco Bay by land and records the first [[central-coast/santa-cruz-mountains\|redwoods]] |
| 1775 | The *San Carlos* sails through the Golden Gate and anchors at [[bay-area/angel-island\|Angel Island]] |
| 1775 to 1776 | Juan Bautista de Anza leads 240 colonists across the desert. See [[desert/anza-borrego\|Anza-Borrego]] |
| 1787 | Mission [[central-coast/la-purisima\|La Purísima]] is founded |
| 1797 | Mission San José is founded below [[bay-area/mission-peak\|Mission Peak]] |
| 1812 | Russians build [[north-coast/fort-ross\|Fort Ross]]. An earthquake destroys the first La Purísima |
| 1821 | Mexico wins independence from Spain |
| 1824 | The Chumash revolt |
| 1833 to 1834 | Mexico closes the missions |
| 1841 | The Russians sell Fort Ross to John Sutter |
| 1844 | The first wagons cross [[sierra-nevada/donner-pass\|Donner Pass]] |
| 1846 | American settlers raise the Bear Flag at Sonoma. The United States and Mexico go to war. The Donner Party is trapped in the snow |

## Gold and statehood

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| 1848 | January 24: gold at [[gold-country/coloma\|Coloma]]. February 2: Mexico cedes California. See [[big-picture/gold-rush\|The Gold Rush]] |
| 1849 | The forty-niners arrive. Lost emigrants name [[desert/death-valley\|Death Valley]] |
| 1850 | California becomes the thirty-first state. The *Frolic* wrecks on the [[north-coast/mendocino-coast\|Mendocino coast]] |
| 1851 | The Mariposa Battalion enters [[sierra-nevada/yosemite-valley\|Yosemite Valley]]. Eighteen treaties are signed and later buried. See [[big-picture/first-peoples\|First Peoples]] |
| 1852 | Giant sequoias are made known to the world. The [[bay-area/oakland-redwoods\|Oakland redwoods]] are being cut |
| 1853 | Hydraulic mining is invented near Nevada City |
| 1859 | Gold is found at [[eastern-sierra/bodie\|Bodie]] and silver at the Comstock Lode, which strips the forests of [[sierra-nevada/lake-tahoe\|Tahoe]] |
| 1864 | Lincoln grants Yosemite Valley and [[sierra-nevada/mariposa-grove\|Mariposa Grove]] to California as a public park |
| 1867 | Chinese railroad workers finish the Summit Tunnel at [[sierra-nevada/donner-pass\|Donner Pass]] |
| 1868 | John Muir arrives in California. The Hayward Fault earthquake |
| 1869 | The transcontinental railroad is completed |
| 1870 | The [[bay-area/point-reyes\|Point Reyes]] Lighthouse is lit |
| 1872 | The Lone Pine earthquake. See [[eastern-sierra/mount-whitney\|Mount Whitney]] |
| 1872 to 1873 | The Modoc War at [[volcano-country/lava-beds\|Lava Beds]] |
| 1873 | Three fishermen climb Mount Whitney |
| 1875 | George Anderson climbs [[sierra-nevada/half-dome\|Half Dome]] |
| 1879 | Bodie's boom peaks |
| 1882 | The Chinese Exclusion Act |
| 1884 | The Sawyer Decision ends hydraulic mining at [[gold-country/malakoff-diggins\|Malakoff Diggins]] |

## Parks and water

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| 1890 | Sequoia and Yosemite National Parks are created. See [[sierra-nevada/giant-forest\|Giant Forest]] |
| 1892 | The Sierra Club is founded, with Muir as president. Eight elephant seals are found alive. See [[central-coast/ano-nuevo\|Año Nuevo]] |
| 1896 | The Sutro Baths open at [[bay-area/lands-end\|Lands End]]. The railroad up [[bay-area/mount-tamalpais\|Mount Tamalpais]] opens |
| 1902 | [[central-coast/big-basin\|Big Basin]] becomes the first state park |
| 1905 | The first Dipsea Race. The Colorado River breaks loose and fills the Salton Sea. Jack London buys his [[bay-area/jack-london\|ranch]] |
| 1906 | The San Francisco earthquake and fire |
| 1908 | [[bay-area/muir-woods\|Muir Woods]] and [[central-coast/pinnacles\|Pinnacles]] become national monuments |
| 1910 | The immigration station opens on [[bay-area/angel-island\|Angel Island]] |
| 1911 | Ishi walks out of the hills. [[eastern-sierra/devils-postpile\|Devils Postpile]] is saved from dynamite |
| 1913 | The Los Angeles Aqueduct opens. Congress approves the Hetch Hetchy dam. See [[big-picture/water\|Water]]. Wolf House burns. Death Valley records 134 degrees |
| 1914 to 1917 | [[volcano-country/lassen\|Lassen Peak]] erupts |
| 1916 | The National Park Service is created |
| 1918 | The Save the Redwoods League is founded. See [[north-coast/humboldt-redwoods\|Humboldt Redwoods]] |
| 1919 | Cables go up on Half Dome |
| 1933 | [[central-coast/point-lobos\|Point Lobos]] becomes a state reserve |
| 1933 to 1942 | The Civilian Conservation Corps builds trails and rebuilds La Purísima |
| 1936 | Joshua Tree National Monument, thanks to [[desert/joshua-tree\|Minerva Hoyt]] |
| 1937 | Highway 1 opens through [[central-coast/big-sur\|Big Sur]]. The Golden Gate Bridge opens |
| 1940 | [[sierra-nevada/kings-canyon\|Kings Canyon]] National Park |
| 1941 | Los Angeles begins diverting the streams of [[eastern-sierra/mono-lake\|Mono Lake]] |
| 1942 to 1945 | Japanese Americans are incarcerated at [[eastern-sierra/manzanar\|Manzanar]] |

## Since 1945

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| 1957 | Methuselah is dated. The northwest face of Half Dome is climbed |
| 1962 | Point Reyes National Seashore. [[eastern-sierra/bodie\|Bodie]] becomes a state park |
| 1963 | The [[desert/san-jacinto\|Palm Springs tram]] opens. The Tall Trees are measured |
| 1964 | The Wilderness Act. Floods devastate the Eel River |
| 1968 | [[north-coast/redwood-national-park\|Redwood National Park]]. The first prescribed burns in the sequoias. See [[big-picture/fire\|Fire]] |
| 1969 | The first Manzanar pilgrimage |
| 1970 | The King Range, on the [[north-coast/lost-coast\|Lost Coast]], becomes the first National Conservation Area |
| 1978 | Tule elk return to Point Reyes. The Mono Lake Committee is founded |
| 1980 | [[south-coast/channel-islands\|Channel Islands]] National Park |
| 1983 | The public trust ruling on Mono Lake |
| 1987 | The last wild condor is captured for breeding |
| 1988 | Congress apologizes for the wartime incarceration |
| 1989 | The Loma Prieta earthquake |
| 1994 | The California Desert Protection Act creates Death Valley and Joshua Tree National Parks and [[desert/kelso-dunes\|Mojave National Preserve]] |
| 2006 | Hyperion, the tallest known tree, is found |
| 2013 | Pinnacles becomes a national park |
| 2016 | Island foxes come off the endangered list |
| 2019 | California formally apologizes to its Native peoples |
| 2020 | Record fires burn Big Basin, the sequoia groves and the Joshua trees of Cima Dome |
| 2022 | The Yurok Tribe releases condors on the north coast |
| 2023 | A lake returns to Death Valley, and Tulare Lake to the Central Valley |
| 2024 | The Klamath River dams come out |
