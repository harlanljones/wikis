# Gold Country

The western foothills of the Sierra Nevada, from Mariposa north to the Yuba River, are oak savanna, red dirt and small towns with iron shutters on the old brick buildings. Highway 49, named for the forty-niners, strings them together. For a few years in the middle of the 1800s this was the most talked-about place on Earth.

The three hikes here make a neat set: the place where the Gold Rush started, a place that shows what was here before it, and a place that shows how it ended.
