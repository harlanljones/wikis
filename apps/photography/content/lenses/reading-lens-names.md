# Reading lens names

A lens name such as **Sony FE 70-200mm F2.8 GM OSS II** or **Nikon NIKKOR Z DX 16-50mm f/3.5-6.3 VR** packs the mount, sensor coverage, focal length, aperture, quality tier and features into one line. Once you know the pattern, they are easy to read.

## The pattern

Most names follow this order:

1. **Brand and mount**: which cameras it fits
2. **Focal length** in millimetres
3. **Maximum aperture**
4. **Tier and features**: abbreviations for quality level, stabilization, motor type and generation

Taking **Canon RF 24-105mm F4 L IS USM** apart:

| Part | Meaning |
| ---- | ------- |
| RF | Canon RF mount, covers full frame |
| 24-105mm | Zoom from 24mm to 105mm |
| F4 | Maximum aperture f/4 throughout |
| L | Canon's professional tier |
| IS | Optical image stabilization |
| USM | Ultrasonic focus motor |

## Marks on the lens itself

- **Ø67**, **Ø77**, **Ø82**: the filter thread diameter in millimetres. See [[lenses/filters|filters]].
- **0.25m / 0.82ft**: the minimum focus distance.
- **1:1**, **1:2**: maximum magnification. 1:1 is life size on the sensor.
- **AF/MF** switch, a focus limiter (**FULL / 3m-∞**) on telephotos, and stabilization mode switches.

## Things names do not tell you

- **Whether an APS-C lens fits a full-frame body.** On mirrorless mounts it does, and the camera crops the image automatically. On Canon's DSLRs, EF-S lenses do not fit full-frame bodies.
- **Weight.** A 50mm f/1.2 can weigh five times as much as a 50mm f/1.8.
- **Whether a zoom extends** when you zoom. Internal zoom lenses stay the same length.
