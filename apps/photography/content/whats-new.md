# What changed in 2026

A running summary of notable camera releases, so the rest of the wiki can be read in context. Last checked on **10 November 2026**.

2026 was a quieter year than 2025. The first half brought almost nothing from Nikon or Fujifilm, and the main releases clustered in May and September.

## What these releases mean

**High resolution no longer means slow.** Stacked sensors have reached the 60-megapixel class. Until now, choosing resolution meant accepting slower bursts and more [[cameras/shutters|rolling shutter]]. See [[cameras/sensor-technology|sensor technology]].

**Stabilization has reached the entry level of full frame.** With the Nikon Z5 II, Z5IIC and Canon EOS R8 Mark II, a stabilized full-frame body costs $1,400 to $1,900. See [[buying/by-budget|cameras by budget]].

**Viewfinderless full-frame bodies are now a category.** The Nikon Z5IIC and Canon EOS R6 V join the Sony ZV-E1, Panasonic Lumix S9 and Sigma BF. They suit video and travel. For stills in bright light, a [[cameras/viewfinders-and-screens|viewfinder]] is still worth having.

**Video-first siblings.** Makers increasingly sell the same sensor in a stills body and a video body: Canon R6 Mark III and R6 V, Nikon Z6 III and ZR, Sony a7 IV and FX2.

## Expected but not announced

These have been widely reported but were **not official** when this page was last checked. Treat everything about them as rumour.

| Camera | Status |
| ------ | ------ |
| Fujifilm X-T6 | Reported for September, then November, then delayed again. Rumoured to keep a 40-megapixel sensor with a new processor |
| Canon EOS R7 Mark II | Long rumoured with a stacked APS-C sensor |
| Nikon Z9 II | The most persistent Nikon rumour |
| Sony a7S IV, Sony a6900 | Speculated, with no firm reports |

Do not hold off a purchase for a rumoured camera unless you have a specific need that only it would meet. Announcement dates slip, launch stock is short, and launch prices are the highest a camera will ever have.

## Prices

US prices rose through 2025 as import tariffs were passed on, in some cases by 10 to 15 percent over launch prices. Prices elsewhere were less affected. The launch prices quoted across this wiki are a guide to where a camera sits in the range. Check a retailer for the current figure.
