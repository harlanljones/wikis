# Sony E

Sony's **E mount** dates from 2010 and went full frame in 2013 with the original a7, which makes it the oldest of the full-frame mirrorless systems. Full-frame lenses are labelled **FE** and APS-C lenses **E**. Sony also makes most of the image sensors used by other camera brands.

## What Sony is known for

- **The largest lens selection.** A long head start and an open mount mean more native lenses than any other mirrorless system: Sony's own, plus the full ranges from Sigma, Tamron, Viltrox, Samyang and others. There is usually a choice at three price points for any focal length.
- **Autofocus.** Sony's real-time tracking set the standard that others have chased, and recent bodies have a dedicated processor for subject recognition.
- **Compact full frame.** The a7C series puts a full-frame sensor in a body the size of an APS-C camera.
- **Sensor technology.** Sony introduced the first full-frame stacked sensor (a9, 2017) and the first full-frame global shutter (a9 III, 2023).
- **Video.** The FX cinema line and ZV vlogging line share the mount, and S-Log3 and S-Cinetone are industry staples.

## What to watch for

- **Menus and handling.** Much improved since 2021, but still dense. Grips are smaller than Canon's or Nikon's.
- **Third-party limits.** Sony caps non-Sony lenses at 15 fps and does not allow third-party teleconverters. This matters only on the fastest bodies.
- **A confusing range.** Sony keeps older generations on sale, so several versions of the same camera are sold side by side.

## How the names work

| Series | Meaning |
| ------ | ------- |
| a7 | The mainstream full-frame line |
| a7R | High resolution |
| a7S | Low resolution, optimised for video and low light |
| a7C | Compact bodies with a corner viewfinder |
| a9 | Speed, for sport |
| a1 | Flagship: speed and resolution together |
| a6xxx | APS-C |
| ZV | Vlogging bodies with no viewfinder |
| FX | Cinema line |

Roman numerals mark the generation, so an a7 V is the fifth a7.

## Which one

- **First camera on a budget:** a6100 or a6400 with a Sigma or Tamron zoom.
- **Best APS-C:** a6700.
- **Travel and everyday full frame:** a7C II.
- **One body for everything:** a7 V. A discounted a7 IV remains a fair buy for slower subjects.
- **Landscapes and studio:** a7R V. The a7R VI adds speed for wildlife and action at high resolution.
- **Sport:** a9 III or a1 II.

## A-mount

Sony's older A mount, inherited from Minolta and used on its DSLR-style cameras, was discontinued in 2021. A-mount lenses adapt to E-mount bodies.
