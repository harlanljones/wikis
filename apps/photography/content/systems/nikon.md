# Nikon Z

Nikon's mirrorless system uses the **Z mount**, introduced in 2018. It has the widest opening and the shortest flange distance of any full-frame mount. Full-frame bodies and lenses are called **FX** and APS-C ones **DX**.

## What Nikon is known for

- **Value.** Nikon has priced its recent bodies aggressively. The Z5 II and Z50 II offer flagship-derived autofocus at entry-level prices.
- **S-Line lenses.** The f/1.8 S primes and the f/4 and f/2.8 S zooms are consistently among the sharpest available.
- **Lightweight telephotos.** Phase Fresnel lenses such as the 600mm f/6.3 VR S and 800mm f/6.3 VR S are a fraction of the weight of traditional designs, and the 180-600mm is a well-priced wildlife zoom.
- **Handling.** Deep grips, good weather sealing and logical controls, carried over from its DSLRs.
- **Video.** Nikon bought the cinema camera maker RED in 2024. The Z6 III, Z8 and Z9 record raw video internally, and the ZR brings RED's colour and codec to a $2,200 body.
- **F-mount heritage.** DSLR lenses with built-in motors work fully on the FTZ adapter.

## What to watch for

- **DX lenses.** The native APS-C lens range is small and mostly slow zooms, though the Z DX 24mm f/1.7 and Sigma's f/1.4 primes help.
- **Third-party lenses** are licensed selectively. Tamron zooms and Viltrox primes are available. Sigma's full-frame lenses have not been, so far.
- **High resolution below the Z8.** The 45-megapixel Z7 II dates from 2020 and has not been replaced.

| Camera | Released | Megapixels | Notes | Launch price |
| ------ | -------- | ---------- | ----- | ------------ |
| Z30 | 2022 | 20.9 | No viewfinder, aimed at vlogging | $710 |

None of the DX bodies has in-body stabilization, so choose VR lenses.

## Which one

- **First camera:** Z50 II.
- **First full-frame camera:** Z5 II. It is hard to find a reason to spend more unless you need speed.
- **Smallest full frame:** Z5IIC with the 26mm f/2.8 or 40mm f/2.
- **Action, events and hybrid video:** Z6 III.
- **Landscapes, wildlife and everything else at a high level:** Z8.
- **If you love the look of a film camera:** Z f or Z fc.
- **Mostly video:** ZR.
