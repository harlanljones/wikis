# L-Mount

The L-Mount was created by Leica in 2014. In 2018 Leica, Panasonic and Sigma formed the **L-Mount Alliance** to share it. Any L-Mount lens works on any L-Mount body with full function, whichever member made it.

The alliance has since grown to include DJI, Blackmagic Design, Samyang, Sirui, Viltrox and others. The mount covers full frame and APS-C, though almost all current products are full frame.

## What L-Mount is known for

- **Choice across brands.** Panasonic makes the mainstream bodies, Leica the luxury ones, and Sigma the unconventional ones. Sigma's complete Art, Contemporary and Sports lens ranges are native.
- **Video.** Panasonic's bodies offer open gate recording, internal raw, unlimited recording with built-in fans, and Real Time LUTs. Blackmagic cinema cameras use the mount too.
- **Value.** The Lumix S5 II is one of the most fully featured full-frame bodies at its price, and Panasonic's f/1.8 primes are inexpensive.
- **Consistent lenses.** The Lumix S 18, 24, 35, 50 and 85mm f/1.8 primes share the same size, weight and filter thread, so they swap on a gimbal without rebalancing.

## What to watch for

- **Autofocus history.** Panasonic bodies before 2023 used contrast-based DFD autofocus, which pulses in video and struggles with action. The S5 II and later use phase detection and are competitive. Check this when buying used.
- **Action photography.** Tracking and the long telephoto range are improving but trail Canon, Nikon and Sony.
- **Size.** Bodies and lenses lean toward large, apart from the S9 and Sigma's cameras.

## Which one

- **Best value all-rounder:** Lumix S5 II.
- **Hybrid stills and video at a higher level:** Lumix S1 II.
- **High resolution:** Lumix S1R II.
- **Small everyday camera:** Lumix S9 or Sigma BF.
- **If money is no object:** Leica SL3.
