# Photography Wiki

A plain-language guide to modern interchangeable-lens cameras. It covers how mirrorless cameras and DSLRs work, what the numbers on a spec sheet mean, how the major camera systems differ, and how to choose a camera and lenses without overspending.

Model lineups and prices were last checked in **November 2026**. The concepts do not date; the model names do. See [[whats-new|What changed in 2026]] for the most recent releases.

## Three things worth knowing before anything else

1. **Any current camera takes excellent photos.** The differences between a $900 body and a $4,000 body show up at the edges: fast action, very low light, heavy cropping, professional video. For daylight landscapes, travel and family photos, they are close.
2. **You are buying into a lens mount, not a camera.** Bodies are replaced every few years. Lenses are kept for a decade or more, and they only fit one [[systems|system]].
3. **Light is the limiting factor.** Almost every spec is a way of collecting more light, or of coping with too little of it. Once [[basics|exposure]] makes sense, spec sheets do too.
