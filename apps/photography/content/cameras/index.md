# Inside the camera

An interchangeable-lens camera is a sensor, a way to see what the sensor sees, a system for focusing, and a processor that ties them together. This section explains each part and what its specifications mean in use.

## The parts, in order of how much they matter

1. **The lens.** It forms the image. No sensor can recover detail the lens did not deliver. See [[lenses|Lenses]].
2. **[[cameras/sensor-sizes|Sensor size]].** Sets how much light the camera can gather, which drives low-light quality, depth of field, and the size and cost of the whole system.
3. **[[cameras/autofocus|Autofocus]].** The biggest real-world difference between cheap and expensive bodies, and between old and new ones.
4. **[[cameras/sensor-technology|Sensor readout speed]].** Stacked and partially stacked sensors allow fast silent bursts and cleaner video.
5. **[[cameras/image-stabilization|Stabilization]].** Lets you use slower shutter speeds handheld.
6. **[[cameras/megapixels|Megapixels]].** Matters less than the marketing suggests, above about 24.
7. **Handling.** Grip, dials, [[cameras/viewfinders-and-screens|viewfinder]], weather sealing, [[cameras/memory-cards-and-batteries|battery life and card slots]]. Hard to read from a spec sheet, and the reason to hold a camera before buying it.

## A note on generations

Camera technology moves in steps, and a model's age tells you a lot. Roughly:

- **Before 2018.** DSLRs dominate. Mirrorless autofocus is good for still subjects and unreliable for action.
- **2018 to 2021.** Canon, Nikon and Panasonic launch full-frame mirrorless mounts. Eye autofocus becomes dependable.
- **2021 to 2023.** Stacked sensors arrive in flagships (Sony a1, Nikon Z9, Canon R3). Subject detection trained by machine learning spreads down the range.
- **2024 onward.** Subject detection is standard even in entry-level bodies. Partially stacked sensors bring fast readout to mid-range cameras. Pre-capture bursts and in-camera content credentials appear.

A mid-range body from 2024 or later focuses better than a flagship from 2018. That matters when [[buying/buying-used|buying used]].
