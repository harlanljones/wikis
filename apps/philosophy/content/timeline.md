# Timeline

Twenty-four centuries of political thought on one page: when each thinker lived, what they wrote and what was happening around them. Political philosophy is usually written in a crisis, so the events matter as much as the dates.

## Lifespans

Each bar is a life and links to that thinker's page. The two panels are drawn to different scales.

<div data-timeline></div>

Some overlaps are worth noticing. [[thinkers/hobbes|Hobbes]] was alive for [[thinkers/locke|Locke's]] first forty-seven years. [[thinkers/hume|Hume]], [[thinkers/rousseau|Rousseau]], [[thinkers/smith|Smith]] and [[thinkers/kant|Kant]] were born within thirteen years of each other. [[thinkers/tocqueville|Tocqueville]], [[thinkers/mill|Mill]] and [[thinkers/marx|Marx]] were contemporaries who read each other or should have. And there is a gap of nearly eight hundred years between [[thinkers/augustine|Augustine]] and [[thinkers/aquinas|Aquinas]] on this chart, which is a gap in the wiki and not in history.
