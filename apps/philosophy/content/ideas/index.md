# Ideas

These pages cover the concepts that political philosophers argue about. Each one explains the idea in plain terms, sets out the main positions, and links to the thinkers who held them.

If you are new to the subject, start with [[ideas/social-contract|the social contract]] and [[ideas/liberty|liberty]]. Between them they introduce most of the cast.
