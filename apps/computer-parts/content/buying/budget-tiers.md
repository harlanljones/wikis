# Builds by budget

> **Prices on this page were estimated on 10 November 2026** from the figures in [[market|The market right now]]. They are approximate and will drift. Use the part classes as the guide and check current prices on PCPartPicker.

These are balanced desktop part lists at four budgets, excluding the monitor, keyboard, mouse and operating system. Each one names a class of part and an example. Swap in whatever is cheapest in that class on the day.

Before building any of them, price a prebuilt with the same processor and graphics card. In the current market it may well be cheaper. See [[buying/prebuilt-desktops|How to choose a prebuilt desktop]].

A mini PC or a refurbished business desktop does this job for half the price. Build this one only if you want to add a graphics card later.

## Workstation add-ons

For video editing, 3D or heavy development, start from the mid-range or high-end list and change:

| Change | Why | Extra cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Processor to a Ryzen 9 9950X or 9950X3D, or a Core Ultra 7 270K Plus | More cores for exports and renders | $80 to $400 |
| Memory to 64GB | Large projects and timelines | $650 to $850 |
| A second NVMe drive for projects and cache | Keeps working files off the system drive | $250 or more |
| An Nvidia card, if your software favors it | See [[use-cases/creative\|Photo, video and 3D work]] | Varies |

## How to adapt a list

- **Start with the graphics card and the monitor.** They define what the build can do. See [[parts/monitor|Monitor]].
- **Do not trim the power supply.** It is the cheapest insurance in the build.
- **The processor is the easiest place to step down.** In games at 1440p and above, a $180 chip is close to a $480 one with a mid-range graphics card.
- **Storage is the easiest part to add later.** Start with 1TB and add a second drive when you need it.
- **Reuse what you have:** case, power supply, SSDs, memory of the right type, fans.
- **Run the list through PCPartPicker.** It checks compatibility and finds the cheapest retailer for each part. Then use the [[building/compatibility|Compatibility checklist]].
