## The minimum sensible computer

Whatever you do, a computer bought today should have:

- A processor from the current or previous generation.
- **16GB of memory.** 8GB only for a Chromebook or very light use.
- **An SSD** of at least 256GB, and preferably 512GB.
- A screen of **1080p or better**, IPS or OLED, at least 300 nits on a laptop.
- Support for **Windows 11**, a current macOS, or a Chromebook with years of updates left.

Anything below that line is a false economy, because it will feel slow within a year or two.

## If you are buying for someone else

- **A child or student:** a Chromebook or a sturdy mid-range laptop. Check what the school requires. Durability and battery life matter more than speed.
- **An older relative:** whatever matches the system they already know. A large, bright screen and a simple setup matter more than anything on the spec sheet. A mini PC with a 27-inch monitor is easier on the eyes than a laptop.
- **A teenager who wants to game:** see [[use-cases/gaming|Gaming]], and involve them in choosing. A desktop gives far more for the money if it can stay in one room.
- **A small business:** business-class machines with three-year warranties, and a backup plan. See [[parts/storage#Backups|Backups]].
