# The parts

One page per component. Each page starts with a one-line summary and a table of what to buy, then explains every spec you will see on a product listing, then goes deeper for readers who want the detail.

## Inside the computer

| Part | What it decides | How much it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[parts/cpu\|Processor (CPU)]] | How fast general work gets done | High for everyone, but mid-range is enough for most |
| [[parts/gpu\|Graphics card (GPU)]] | Game performance, 3D, video effects, local AI | Everything for gamers. Nothing for office work |
| [[parts/memory\|Memory (RAM)]] | How much you can have open at once | High up to a point, then nothing |
| [[parts/storage\|Storage]] | How much you can keep, and how quickly things open | An SSD is essential. Beyond that, capacity matters more than speed |
| [[parts/motherboard\|Motherboard]] | Which parts fit, and how many | Low for speed. High for compatibility and future upgrades |
| [[parts/power-supply\|Power supply]] | Whether everything gets clean, sufficient power | Low for speed. High for reliability and safety |
| [[parts/cooling\|Cooling]] | Temperature and noise | Moderate. Cheap coolers are very good now |
| [[parts/case\|Case]] | Size, airflow, noise, looks | Low for speed, as long as air can get in |

## Outside the computer

| Part | What it decides | How much it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[parts/monitor\|Monitor]] | Everything you see | High, and often neglected |
| [[parts/peripherals\|Keyboard, mouse and audio]] | Comfort over hours of use | Personal, but real |
| [[parts/networking\|Networking]] | Download speed, video-call quality, online game lag | Often limited by your router, not your computer |

## Where the money should go

As a rough share of a desktop budget, excluding the monitor:

| Build | Processor | Graphics card | Memory and storage | Everything else |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Office and home | 30% | 0% (integrated) | 35% | 35% |
| Gaming | 15% to 20% | 35% to 45% | 20% | 20% |
| Photo and video editing | 25% | 25% | 30% | 20% |
| Programming | 30% | 0% to 10% | 40% | 20% to 30% |

Memory and storage take a larger share than these tables used to show, because both are expensive at the moment. See [[market|The market right now]].

## Reading a part page

Every part page uses the same headings:

- **What it does**, in plain words.
- **What to buy**, as a table by need.
- **The specs, explained.** Each spec is marked with how much it matters.
- **Specs you can mostly ignore.**
- **Going deeper**, for technical readers.
- **Common mistakes.**
